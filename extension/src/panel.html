Foxy Jumpscare

 

Normally Foxy appears over the page you are reading. A settings page, a PDF or the new tab page will not let him in, so this gives him a tab of his own beside it for a second, then puts you back. Off means he waits for an ordinary tab — the countdown is not spent while he waits.

Foxy shows up in all your tabs, so he is still there if you switch mid-scare, but only the one you are looking at makes a sound. On, every open tab screams at once.

Normally the countdown only runs while you are using the browser. On, it also runs while you are at the computer in other apps, so he comes around sooner. He still waits for you to come back: a browser cannot draw over other apps.
